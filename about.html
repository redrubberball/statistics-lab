About Statistics Lab

Statistics Lab is a free collection of interactive tools for learning statistics, checking calculations, and exploring real data. The goal is to help make statistical thinking useful, approachable, and a little more fun.

Who made this?

Developed by Dr. Pridemore as an independently maintained educational project. This website is not an official Valencia College website.

AI & development

AI tools have assisted with programming, design, and drafting explanations. I review, revise, and test the material, and I continue improving it. If you notice an error or something confusing, please send feedback.

Data & reliability

Interactive calculations are intended for learning and checking work, not high-stakes decision-making. Live Stats uses outside data sources. Figures may be delayed, incomplete, or revised; use the original source for official or time-sensitive information. Individual graphs identify the data source and reporting period.

Privacy, accessibility & feedback

The tools can be explored without creating a Statistics Lab account. Feedback is optional and uses an external form; please don't submit names, student IDs, grades, or other private information. Third-party data and form providers may have their own privacy policies.

I'm working to keep the site accessible. If a control, graph, or explanation is difficult to use, please let me know.
