Show the math and assumptions

  • The graph shows the sampling distribution of the test statistic under H₀: Z ~ N(0,1).
  • The test direction must be chosen from the research question before looking at the data.
  • Results presume an appropriate z-test and satisfied assumptions; this explorer does not check them.
  • A P-value is not the probability that H₀ is true. Failing to reject is not accepting H₀.
