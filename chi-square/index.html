Chi-Square Test of Independence

Are the two categorical variables associated?

H₀: The two population variables are independent (not associated).
Hₐ: The two population variables are associated.

Expected counts & calculations

Expected count = (row total × column total) ÷ grand total.

Show expected counts for every cell
Show each contribution to χ²

For each cell: (Observed − Expected)² ÷ Expected. Add all the contributions to obtain the test statistic.

This is always a right-tailed chi-square test. An association does not establish causation.
